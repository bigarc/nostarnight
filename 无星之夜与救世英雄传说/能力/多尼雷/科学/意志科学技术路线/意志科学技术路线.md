# 意志科学技术路线  
**Will-Science Technological Lineage**

坚持意志科学技术路线的研究院是多尼雷最为分散的实体——它没有庞大的厂房，没有集中的总部，而是以原生人为群星散落——原生人，这些历经世界大战幸存、基因未受污染的珍贵人类——他们才是多尼雷真正的创造者，而他们所造之物——机器——反过来护卫着这些脆弱的肉身。技术在这里走向消失：不再需要可见的机械与接口，一切造物都收敛为意志对物质的直接干预。非物质力场、意念操控、以精神重构现实，科学与魔法在此殊途同归。它的美学是极简到近乎空无的寂静——因为力量的源泉已回归于肉身与意志本身。

The Will-Science lineage is Tonile's most dispersed entity—it has no massive factories, no centralized headquarters, but is scattered like stars with Originals at its core.Originals—those precious humans who survived the World War with uncontaminated genes—they are Tonile's true creators, and what they created—machines—in turn guard these fragile bodies. Technology here moves toward disappearance: visible machinery and interfaces are no longer needed; all creations converge into the direct intervention of will upon matter. Non-material force fields, thought manipulation, the reconstruction of reality through spirit—science and magic converge here by different paths. Its aesthetic is a silence reduced to near-emptiness—because the source of power has returned to flesh and will itself.
