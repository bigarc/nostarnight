# 银翼光谱技术路线  
**Silverwing Spectrum Technological Lineage**

专注于用户体验的定制化技术路线，光滑、炫彩、流线形。还包括虚拟现实和增强现实的技术，集中于软件以及相关“微小”的高精技术——也包括相关的个人义体。”装甲“主导曲线与流线形。产品表面呈现类生物釉质的光滑感，色彩采用柔和渐变彩色，材质模拟温润玉石、生物表皮或液态金属。光线在其上散射、漫射。设备通过生物电场、微手势、投影界面甚至意图预读进行交互，取消一切物理按钮与可见接口的可视化与神经意识操作——深度的人本操控——理想的技术应如意识本身般流动，又如梦境本身般真实。

A customized technological lineage focused on user experience—smooth, iridescent, streamlined. It also includes virtual reality and augmented reality technologies, concentrated in software and related "micro" high-precision technologies—including related personal prosthetics. "Armor" is dominated by curves and streamlined forms. Product surfaces present a bio-enamel-like smoothness; colors use soft gradient hues; materials simulate warm jade, biological skin, or liquid metal. Light scatters and diffuses upon them. Devices interact through bioelectric fields, micro-gestures, projection interfaces, and even intent pre-reading, abolishing all physical buttons and visible interfaces in favor of visualization and neural-consciousness operation—deep human-centric control. The ideal technology should flow like consciousness itself, and be as real as a dream itself.
