#  远航主义技术路线  
**Voyager Technological Lineage**

金线谱几何线设计主义、星海孤航主义。巨大、流线型、适合长期航行的舰体。舰船可能带有太阳帆、推进器阵列、深空栖息环。线条比 T 更修长，比 B 更优雅。呈现宁静的深空全景——一艘舰船在黑色虚空中滑行，远处是稀疏的星点，背后是母世界逐渐缩小的光斑。有一种宗教朝圣般的庄严与悲壮。

Golden-line geometry design, lone voyage across the sea of stars. Massive, streamlined hulls suited for long-term voyages. Ships may carry solar sails, thruster arrays, deep-space habitat rings. Lines are more slender than T's, more elegant than B's. It presents a tranquil deep-space panorama—a ship gliding through black void, distant sparse stars, the mother world's light shrinking behind. It carries a solemnity and tragic grandeur like a religious pilgrimage.
