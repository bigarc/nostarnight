# 设计仿生技术路线  
**Design Bionic Technological Lineage**

生物与机械深度共生的构装技术路线，将类骨骼、羽翼的有机形态与工程造物融为一体。装甲与壳体以漂白骨瓷般的灰白作为基底，遍布尖锐骨刺、分叉翼状延展结构，表面流淌、浸染着血色的绯红纹路。 摒弃纯粹几何的直线，大量采用分叉、锐化、向外舒展的羽翼式切面，形体充满锋利的生物解剖感。表面并非光滑无缝，也非裸露管线，而是如同致密钙化骨骼，带有细密的刻理与脊状凸起。 机械的动力组件被埋藏在骨状外壳的缝隙、羽片的夹层之间，极少直接外露。在动力激活时，红色神经传导纹路内部会透出淡淡的血色辉光，顺着骨骼的脉络蔓延流淌。

A construction technology lineage of deep symbiosis between biology and machinery, fusing bone-like and wing-like organic forms with engineered creations. Armor and shells use a bleached bone-porcelain grey-white as their base, covered with sharp bone spurs and forked wing-like extensions, with blood-crimson veins flowing and staining the surface. It rejects pure geometric straight lines, extensively adopting forked, sharpened, outward-stretching wing-like facets; the form is filled with a sharp biological-anatomical sensibility. The surface is neither smooth and seamless nor exposed piping, but rather like dense calcified bone, with fine grain and ridge-like protrusions. Mechanical power components are buried in the crevices of bone-like shells and the layers of feather blades, rarely directly exposed. When power is activated, a faint blood-colored glow shines from within the red neural conduction veins, spreading and flowing along the bone's meridians.
