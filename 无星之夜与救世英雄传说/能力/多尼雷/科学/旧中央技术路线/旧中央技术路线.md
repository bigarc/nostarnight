# 旧中央技术路线  
**Old Central Technological Lineage**

传承自多尼雷世界大战前的“上一个中央政府”的技术路线，建筑与设备呈现极度理性、对称、恢弘的审美。大量使用黑色、灰色及白色，线条清晰冷峻，空间开阔肃穆。光影被严格控制，以营造秩序与宁静感。技术本身不可见，所有机械被完美封装在光滑无缝的黑钢表面之下。装甲呈厚重硬折的哑光深灰或漆黑质感，无任何标识、勋章或装饰性刻痕。所有科技感均内敛于极致的机体线条间隙与瞬间启动时微弱的呼吸点阵辉光中。

Inherited from the technological lineage of the "previous Central Government" before the Tonile World War. Its architecture and equipment present an aesthetic of extreme rationality, symmetry, and grandeur. It uses large amounts of black, grey, and white; lines are clear and cold; spaces are open and solemn. Light and shadow are strictly controlled to create a sense of order and tranquility. Technology itself is invisible—all machinery is perfectly encapsulated beneath smooth, seamless black steel surfaces. Armor has a heavy, hard-folded matte deep grey or pitch-black texture, with no markings, medals, or decorative engravings. All technological sensibility is restrained within the extreme gaps of the body lines and the faint breathing dot-matrix glow at the moment of activation.
