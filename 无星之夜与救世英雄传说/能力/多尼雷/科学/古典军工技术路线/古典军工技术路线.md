# 古典军工技术路线  
**Classical Military-Industrial Technological Lineage**

其技术哲学根植于古典工程理性主义与战略预置思维，大量使用未经修饰的复合装甲、凝灰土、军工陶瓷等材料，呈现深灰色、金属原色、厚涂的沉重现代工业基调。线条笔直、转折锋利，称重结构外露以彰显其强度与可信度——模块化、重工业、效率、可行性还有军事转化预备——“产品”包括基建承包、物流载具、工程设备以及一部分的重型军工武器设施。**中信集团锻造承载灵魂的躯壳与世界本身。**

Rooted in classical engineering rationalism and strategic pre-positioning, it makes heavy use of unadorned composite armor, tuff, and military ceramics, presenting a heavy modern-industrial tone of deep grey and raw metal. Lines are straight, turns are sharp, load-bearing structures are exposed to convey strength and reliability—modularity, heavy industry, efficiency, feasibility, and military conversion readiness. Its "products" include infrastructure contracting, logistics vehicles, engineering equipment, and some heavy military-industrial weapons and facilities. **CCC forges the shell that bears the soul and the world itself.**
