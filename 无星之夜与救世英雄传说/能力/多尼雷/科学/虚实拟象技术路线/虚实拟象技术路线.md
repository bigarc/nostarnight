# 虚实拟象技术路线  
**Virtual-Simulacrum Technological Lineage**

虚实拟象并不追求庞大的工程设备、澎湃的引擎或危险的武器。它的技术形态是虚拟的：网络、表格、制度、关系与服务。面向每个公民，它提供统一店面服务——邻家速递、每日生鲜、时钟代办，将生活本身变成一款定制化的服务软件；面向庞大实体，它输出审计、监理、流程优化的管理机制，以表格与制度渗透进每一个机构的运转。在虚拟世界中，它如同黑客与程序员般无处不在；而在现实世界，其技术体现为一种「侵蚀」——侵入并接管其它实体的装备为己所用，如同幽灵借用生者的躯体。由此而来的「瘦长鬼影」形象——老式电视机的频闪、幽暗光线中拉长的身影——正是其技术的外化。

The Virtual-Simulacrum lineage does not pursue massive engineering equipment, surging engines, or dangerous weapons. Its technological form is virtual: networks, spreadsheets, institutions, relationships, and services. For every citizen, it provides unified storefront services—Neighbor Express, Daily Fresh, Clock Errands—turning life itself into a customizable service software; for massive entities, it outputs management mechanisms of auditing, supervision, and process optimization, permeating the operation of every institution through spreadsheets and institutions. In the virtual world, it is as ubiquitous as hackers and programmers; in the real world, its technology manifests as a kind of "erosion"—invading and taking over other entities' equipment for its own use, like a ghost borrowing the body of the living. The resulting "Slender Man" image—the flicker of old televisions, the elongated figure in dim light—is precisely the externalization of its technology.
