# 前进主义技术路线  
**Progressivist Technological Lineage**

为满足大规模列装需求，潮汛的量产装备遵循裸露的效能主义——彻底摒弃非功能性的光滑外壳与装饰。外露的机械结构、集成管线、动力支架与散热鳍片直接构成其外观，每一处突起与凹陷都服务于明确的散热、减重、维修或升级目的。这种“解剖学般直白”的设计，将效能压榨至极限，并大幅降低了生产与战地维护的复杂度。 与量产品的“减法”相反，试验型号奉行激进的“加法”。每一项甚至是尚不成熟但具有突破性的技术都会被不计成本地堆叠、结合、组织到原型机上，一代又一代原型机在测试中疯狂诞生与陨落。 潮汛军工代表着多尼雷理性中最为激进、不安且充满破坏性创造力的一面，他们的产品整体色调以淡白的功能色为主，如镀灰、镀白、哑光灰白。

To meet the demands of large-scale deployment, Tidewater's mass-produced equipment follows a bare functionalism—completely abandoning non-functional smooth shells and decoration. Exposed mechanical structures, integrated piping, power mounts, and heat sinks directly constitute its appearance; every protrusion and recess serves a clear purpose of heat dissipation, weight reduction, maintenance, or upgrade. This "anatomical bluntness" of design pushes efficiency to the limit and greatly reduces the complexity of production and field maintenance. In contrast to the "subtraction" of mass-produced models, experimental models pursue an aggressive "addition." Every breakthrough technology, even if immature, is stacked, combined, and organized onto prototype machines regardless of cost; generation after generation of prototypes are born and perish madly in testing. Tidewater represents the most radical, restless, and destructively creative side of Tonile's rationality; its products are primarily pale functional colors—ash-plated, white-plated, matte grey-white.
