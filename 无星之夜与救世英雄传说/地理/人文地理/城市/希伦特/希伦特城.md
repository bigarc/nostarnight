
# 希伦特城

---

希伦特，是神的名字。古老的命运女神——玛利亚·希伦特，行走于人间的唯一真神。

希伦特，是帝国的名字。自统一旧大陆以来，它便宣称对已知世界的全部主权。

希伦特，是这座城市的名字。伟大之城的完工被作为历法基准本身。

---
## 第一幕 《筑城记》

**阴谋与战争为荣耀与神的旨意让步**

---
希伦特城的选址由命运亲自决定——古老帝国的故都遗址。在建城的诏令下达之前，魔法学会已在此研究了浮空城废墟十数年。他们测绘断裂的铭文阵列，复原失传的魔导网络，逐步整理那些沉默了数千年的遗落碎片。


帝国需要一座新都城。征服的版图越广，就越需要一个无法被质疑的中心。学院的成果因此被急剧扩大：一座以神为名的城市，同时承担三重职能——新生权力的象征、古老神权的延续、魔法革命的宣告。


旧浮空城被决定化作碎片，重新汇入新都。学院测绘了十数年的精密结构从古老废墟中整体迁移，成为新城市的基址与骨骼，按新时代的需求重新安置。


征集的命令传遍旧大陆，世界上的每一条道路都出现了向新帝都迁徙的人流。朝圣者被拒绝参与内城的建设，便在城市外围聚集下来，一圈，又一圈。开始自发建立一座“外城”——它甚至比内城更早呈现出一座城市的轮廓。学者与冒险家紧随朝圣者的人潮而至。他们听说帝国在这座新城建立了前所未有的魔法学会与真正意义上的大学。他们还听说，古老的浮空城遗迹不会被封锁——它将开放给所有人研究与探索。


为了方便统一规划与协调，学院在这一时期编写了《具现标识要求与规范》。这份文件后来被反复修订，最终成为通行世界的《施法标准及规范》。但在当时，它只有一个目的——让数万名来自不同学派、操着不同术语、水平各异的法师，能够在同一张法阵上协同工作。


建城的正式起点是机关历718年，也就是命运历前82年。魔法学院首先完成了新城的总规划与法阵基址设计。这将不是一座从地基开始、由石匠和劳工慢慢垒起的城市，而是一座从大魔法阵的核心开始、由法师一寸一寸编织而成的城市。整个基址涵盖了一座浮空城规模的魔法构造，即使是基础建设，也至少需要中级法师参与。


内城工程推进到中期时，外城的混乱已经无法忽视。吟游诗人学院——魔法大学的一部分，由早期的学会与学派改组而成——派出了一支规划团队，接手外城的改造。


到命运历44年，旧外城的改造正式完成，未来扩张的规划也同时确定——城市不会停止生长，但从此以后的每一环扩张，都将按照这套规划留足余量地推进。


> *关于历法：按照教会之间的旧规，历法每一千年轮替一次。而命运为这座城市的诞生亲自开口，机关历于800年提前终结，命运历开启。内城完工的年份被定为命运历零年，作为历法的新基准。*


希伦特城开工之时，帝国正处在全面战争的轨道上。征服计划以旧大陆为起点向外辐射，军队、后勤、行政、魔法工业——整个国家机器围绕战争运转，这一状态已持续了相当长的时间。建城的命令改变了这一切。


命运亲自为新城选址，意味着神权的意志再次下达旨意，这一次她直接介入了帝国的扩张进程。大量原本用于前线的高阶法师被抽调至选址地参与建设。魔法学会的重心也从战争法术与魔法工业转向城市规划与大法阵的改造。


对一个处于战争中的帝国而言，建造一座全新的都城，本身就是一种资源上的巨大转移。工匠、学者、法师、物资——这些原本可以投入战场的力量，被成批地调往同一个方向。征服的节奏因此放缓。


> 他们说我不用再上前线了，我该感到庆幸。我已经受够了雨林的潮湿与燥热，还有那些木兽。虽然不知道回国又是要干什么，愿我至少能安稳度过海上这两月。


没有人知道命运为什么在这个时间点做出这个决定。她从不主动解释自己的意图，她的命令只是被颁布，然后被贯彻。帝国是她的造物，她缔造了这个国家，也因此在任何时候都有权重新定义它的方向。皇帝——名义上的最高统治者——在建城这件事上承担的是执行者的角色。他的权威来自军事上的统率力，而当神直接开口，军事领袖便退回到他本来的位置。


对于那些正在前线推进的将领，对于那些已在征服计划中投入了巨大资源的贵族家族，这一转向并非没有阻力。即将被征服地区的旧统治者也心存犹疑，投诚的进程因建城所传递的信号而停缓，甚至是陷入更大的混乱。但命运之神不是遥不可及的。她是重新眷顾“人”的神，是在黑铁时代降临、结束千年战争的存在。帝国的缔造者们中有相当一部分曾与她同行过。质疑一个传说是一回事，质疑一个站在你面前、真实的神则是另一回事。反对者所做的，更多是门户之间的私计与暗中的博弈——一群工于算计的人在时代的转向中试图保住自己位置的努力。


因此，帝国的战争机器没有停止运转，但它的重心发生了偏移。征服仍在继续，但资源、关注和象征意义，都在向那座正在建设中的神名之城倾斜。旧大陆的各个势力注意到了这一点。尚未被征服的地区注意到了这一点。仍在抵抗的敌人也注意到了这一点。


敌人获得了喘息，世界统一的时间又被延后。但命运从未犯过错，希伦特也对她无比忠诚。

### 内城


>**帝国试图在破碎的世界上建立新秩序，但各族群、各教会、新旧势力之间的博弈愈发复杂。与 “多尼雷” 等异世界文明的接触，带来了全新的技术、思想和社会模式，对希伦特的传统造成了巨大冲击。**


内城呈圆形，中央高塔为全城魔法阵的控制中心与阵眼。六条主干道从中央高塔辐射而出，分别通向六座城门。正南为朝圣大道，贯通典礼广场，从正南门直通命运高塔，终至万神殿。正北为离开之路，从大教堂侧后分为两路，绕过教堂群后汇聚为一条大道，通向正北门。东北连接内城港口，含海港与北侧空港高塔，现为军用及学院专用。东南连接外城港口，两者相连但设军事隔离，必要时可统一启用。西北贯通正义大教堂，西南连接崇死教会教友区，正西通往学院区——这三条道路出内城后均接入外城。


内城另有环绕于中央高塔的环城高架道路，依次贯通命运高塔以及枢机院、正义大教堂、崇死教堂、大书库、学院高塔与空港塔楼。环路与中央高塔的六条辐射主干道相交处设有环岛枢纽，可由此切换道路。


内城的地面区域依方位分布。中央高塔由魔法学会与希伦特大学负责维护。其东侧为命运高塔，是命运教会的教堂群、枢机院与万神殿所在地，也是朝圣大道的终点。命运高塔西北侧为正义大教堂，被西北主干道贯通。西南为崇死教会区域，包括教堂群与教友区。南至东北大片区域为学院区，包括大书库、学院城堡群、法师塔群、学院议会与学院高塔，中央高塔亦在其管理范围内，现在同样包括东北沿海的内城港口与内城空港。


内城仍保留有古老浮空城的地上城墙。居住区规划也仍旧沿用，但目前内城仍仅维持居住功能的建筑主要为旅馆、旅店及少数别墅性质的独栋仿古住宅，供来访学者、外交使节与高级神职人员居留。


内城的基础设施骨架同样并非全由新建而成。旧浮空城的排水网络、主干道构型及供能网络被沿用至今——现今中央高塔通路的六辐结构即来源于此。浮空符文阵列亦被完整修复并保留。理论上内城具备整体浮空的能力。


内城地下部分大致分为两层。按区域划分：崇死教堂群下方为地下大墓穴，属教会管辖；大书库下方为地下大书库，规模超过地上部分；学院区下方为学院静室与大教室，用于营造模拟环境，如悬浮海、幽暗地域等。三者基本三等分地下空间，中央控制群位于正中央——即中央高塔正下方，是全城魔法阵的核心控制汇总节点。深层为城市功能的支撑层，排列着重重叠叠的大小法阵与供能炉心，形态为巨大立柱与法阵圆盘，持续回旋运转、机械拼叠，依照预设程序驱动地面城市的各项功能，如魔能灯、具现标识、动态景观、轨道交通等。


内城规划初期，为各古老教会依盟约预留了位置。但随着城市发展，部分教会因无常驻内城的实际需要，其预留区域功能逐渐改变，多数并入大学：命运教会在内城占据核心位置，即命运高塔与枢机院。正义教会保有正义大教堂。崇死教会保有教堂群、公墓群及地下大墓穴。魔法教会即大学本身，他们同时是整座城市的法阵维护者。颂乐教会并入大学，转变为吟游诗人学院。重塑教会在大学内保留学院与议会名额，其总部——即承担医疗职能的大医院——迁往外城。风暴教会原划分内城港口区域，随后迁往外城港口。自然教会在大学内保留学院与议会名额，其人员本身多位于荒野。


### 外城

> **统合、探索、冲突与认同危机，秩序的重建与认知边疆的爆炸性拓展。这是一个充满无限可能，也充满无限危险的全新篇章。**


**第三环**


最早抵达的是一支来自崇死教会的建筑教派，后来被称为“筑城学派”。他们以大理石与神学美学见长，是传统上宏大圣殿的营造者。这支教派在希伦特城的计划阶段便已参与，为城市奠定了最初的规划逻辑与美学基础。经协商，学派的法师与牧师留在内城参与魔法基建，传统工匠则留在城外。这批工匠参与了古城墙的维护，并在内城道路向外延伸的地段规划了最早的居住区，同时建起若干教堂，用于接待后续抵达的朝圣者。这些聚落后来发展为外城的雏形。


随着建城消息传开，更多朝圣者陆续到来，以命运教会的信徒为最多，其中也有来自远方的精灵。与其他种族不同，精灵保留了一定的聚居倾向，在第三环西北侧形成了一条相对集中的街区——并不封闭，但精灵的商铺与语言在此处更为常见。其余种族正常混居。随后到来的是工程承包商与工团，带来了更多劳动力与物资流通。第三环的人口在这一时期迅速膨胀。


吟游诗人学院接手外城改造后，第三环被纳入第一批梳理范围。改造保留了早期工匠规划的道路骨架，将其拓宽并纳入环城道路系统——“环”的称呼即由此而来。教堂与广场作为地标被保留。主要的新增规范是临古城墙一侧的建筑限高，不得超过城墙顶部。建筑外观以红砖与棕色坡瓦为主，楼层普遍在五至六层，沿街立面整齐连续。公寓是最普遍的住宅形式。重塑教会的大医院搬迁至了此处。


如今第三环是外城的主要居住区。它的形状呈月牙形，环绕内城的陆地边界，临海一侧为港口区而空缺。出内城的西北、西南与正西三条主干道在此交汇，分别通往正义大教堂方向、崇死教区方向与学院区方向。沿主干道分布着早期建立的几座教堂，其附属的小型广场是本地居民日常聚集之处。由于希伦特大学的学生直接居住在内城，第三环不承担学生住宿功能。


**第四环**


第四环比第三环更为庞大，依功能划分为多个区域。


以从内城正南大道延伸出的中央大道为界，东侧靠海地区为港区。这里包括旧港口与新式的综合港口。风暴教会将其教堂与观星台从内城搬迁至此，渊洋教会的远洋公会与小教堂亦在此处落脚——后者在世界各地的港口均设有分支，希伦特城自然不会例外。港区还包括为加固海岸与防潮而增修的大坝，以及民用大型空港。数座空港高塔及其扇叶平台覆盖了港口上方，曾有人抱怨这遮蔽了阳光，但也承认风浪一同被挡住了。


中央大道以西是工坊区与商会驻地。冒险者公会在此设立了总部。金龙银行、莴苣藤蔓与怪奇怪奇等商号均在此经营，亦有诸多私人工坊。来自大学的新思想在此处付诸实践，传统的需求则在流水线上批量产出。


第四环是希伦特城的经济中心——也是世界的经济中心。由于不限制建筑高度，商会与工坊竞相将楼房建得更高，以俯瞰港口或远眺内城的高塔群。具现标识被广泛应用于建筑外立面——商号的标志以光与色彩的形式悬浮于楼体表面，价格与公告实时刷新，入夜后整片街区被魔能荧光映照如昼。这种景象在希伦特世界尚属唯一：工厂的产出、商会的订单、港口的吞吐量——这些实实在在的东西支撑着第四环人工密林。对从多尼雷来的观察者而言，这里有一种熟悉的怀念感；对希伦特本地人而言，这便是改革之后“新时代”的面孔。


**第五环**


第五环是外城最外层的环带，仍在持续扩张中。不过它隐约被划分为南北两个截然不同的区域。


北部是广袤的田地。这些农田自建城之前便已存在，世代耕作于此的农夫被统一搬迁，安置在远离内城的规划区域内。他们仍旧耕种着，也许会在劳作之余凝望远方那座不断生长的巨城——那不是他们所理解的世界。又或者，他们的孩子会在多年后以学生的身份踏入先祖曾生活过的地方。这便是新的希伦特城与旧世界之间沉默的割裂。


南部是巨大的工业区。不同于第四环精密的私人炼金工坊，这里的职能是船坞、魔像铸造所与大型工厂化生产设施。世界上最先进的空艇与工程魔像——无论是实物还是概念——在这里完成最后的装配，装船远航至世界各地。


比四环更便宜且更优质的土地吸引着有野心的人持续向外扩张。希伦特大学是这座城市的中心，也是世界上最大的野心家产地。那些学生怀抱着新奇的想法与超凡者一贯的行动力，将第五环视为想法的试验场。更高的高塔在这里立起，更远的边界从这里向外推移。

## 第二幕《朝圣者们》

> **朝圣者们从世界各地汇聚，只为目睹失落了千年的真神。**
> **只要她站立在那里，世间就不再会有战争**

---

**旧船坞**


他们从第五环南部的工业区入城。


最先进入视野的是半空中的空艇骨架，巨大的弧形肋梁被灰铁的蚁群环绕，尚未蒙上皮壳，露出内部的龙骨结构。地面上排列着半成品的工业魔像，关节处还敞开着，等待下一步装配。这里没有植被——沙土地面在干燥的天气里扬起细尘，落在朝圣者的衣袍与赤脚上。工人们就在这片尘土中劳作。他们本身也是信徒。有人停下手中的活，递来水囊与干粮；有人只是站在钢铁的屋檐角下沉默地注视——他们记得自己第一次从这里走过时的样子。


**高塔群**


穿过工业区后进入第四环。朝圣者们从未见过如此繁华的街市。南方诸王国最繁荣的城邦、旧萨赫拉帝国的黄金都城，没有一处能与此地相比。甚至有人想起了古书中关于精灵王朝的描述——那个建造了浮空城的白银纪元，传说中凡人最接近神的时代——即便是那个时代，也未必比得上眼前的景象。朝圣者们将这些归结于神。他们注意到街边一些商号的名称——可能在故乡见过相同的名字——但在这里，同样的名字挂在数倍高的门面上，变得高不可攀。


**大礼拜堂**


沿朝圣大道继续向北，进入第三环。路旁出现了第一座崇死教堂。这是朝圣者在希伦特城见到的第一座大教堂。教堂正门两侧各有一尊石像鬼。左侧是玛德琳·《圣母蒙难》，右侧是博纳罗蒂·《新筑城记》。


《圣母蒙难》的题材是固定的——它雕刻的是生命女神阿加什受难的故事，是正神教会最古老的叙事之一。传统上，《圣母蒙难》会选择一位以慈悲事业终老的女性圣人作为灵魂的承载者。而《筑城记》则以城市建造的历程为题材，由教会内部的大雕塑师中选拔一人执锤。


但希伦特城的外城礼拜教堂并未完全遵循这一惯例。在《筑城记》上，筑城教派改动了题材，而在《圣母蒙难》上，主脉则将它推向了极致。


希伦特城的玛德琳·《圣母蒙难》堪称繁美主义的巅峰之作。整尊雕像据称雕刻了三百余位人物，从每一位天使翅膀上的羽片到利剑上缠绕的荆棘，从橄榄叶的脉络到葡萄藤架的卷须，再到天堂尽头翻涌的腾云——所有细节皆以大理石呈现，不留一处空白。最令人驻足的是圣母的三幅容颜：主位的垂泪，侧位的睡眠与欢笑——石料被匠人呕心沥血地处理出了如同“蒙纱”般的质感，仿佛就真正覆在其面庞之上。


这便是繁美主义的追求：用三百个人物去讲述一个史诗，用一千道凿痕去还原神的荣耀。


而在筑城教派看来，这样的作品不是在荣耀阿加什，而是在荣耀雕塑师自己。


于是他们拿出了博纳罗蒂·《新筑城记》。筑城教派认为希伦特城的建造不应被框定在传统的格式中，便重新拟定了题材，并跳过教会的内部选拔，将方案直接呈送至命运女神面前。女神选中了它。


博纳罗蒂被安置在教堂正门另一侧。它的风格与左侧的玛德琳截然相反：形体以粗粝的几何块面构成，凿痕外露，大理石的天然纹理被完整保留，没有天使，没有腾云，没有任何一个多余的人物。那一双双无面孔的手所托起的城市——只余石头本身的肃穆与力量。它所承载的灵魂亦不寻常——据说是一位筑城学派的大雕塑师。博纳罗蒂极为安静，常年静默于门口，极少移动。


至于玛德琳本人——你不太容易在教堂门口找到她。她是活跃的人，日常在教区探访、在神学课上授课、同孩子说话。三百余位人物的壮丽史诗伴随着慈祥的老者，先贤将其知识直接贯彻。


**古城墙**


朝圣者们在此歇脚，继续向北，抵达内城的古城墙。


城墙的石料带有大理石般的质地，但表面覆盖着风格截然不同的纹饰——叶脉、模仿花瓣的曲线、缠绕的藤蔓图案——这些雕刻不属于崇死教会，不属于人类。它们来自更古老的年代，是白银纪元精灵王朝的遗存。筑城学派所追摹的“古”。


精灵王朝覆灭之后，世界进入了黑铁时代。各个王国、各个宗教、各个种族之间陷入了漫长的战争。人类、精灵、矮人以及亚人——没有一方能够终结这场混战。然后命运女神降临了。她眷顾了希伦特——一个当时并不起眼的人类王国。希伦特在她的庇护下迅速崛起。


**典礼广场**


穿过正南门，沿大道一路向北，绕过中央高塔的环形岛，便抵达了枢机院前的典礼广场。


广场上没有雕像，空间被留出来允许所有人见证。命运女神结束了诸教派之间的纷争，又带领希伦特终结了类人诸族的内战。在那之后，是对异族的征服。而当魔界坠落、魔王降世时，命运赐福了勇者——每一任勇者都是在此处，在这片广场上接受赐福，然后奔赴与魔王的宿命之战。


**命运高塔**


广场北侧便是命运高塔。朝圣者们进入高塔，首先穿过枢机院。


命运教会是帝国的另外半副行政骨架。在偏远的村落，驻村牧师的话语往往比领主的命令更具分量；在城镇，城市主教对应着每一级世俗行政，如同村长与市长之外另有一层并行不悖的治理体系。枢机院则是这套体系的中枢，直接受命于皇帝，统辖军政与经济的调度执行。


命运教会之外，承担具体社会职能的教会亦不在少数。崇死教会管理墓园与丧葬——每一座教堂地下的墓穴，以及世界各地的送葬人与雕塑师们。重塑教会则以医疗为本，其大医院坐落于第三环，苦痛与慈悲同为其教义的底色。


**万神殿堂**


沿高塔继续上行，进入万神殿。


万神殿是一座环形大厅，中央置有一张圆桌。围绕圆桌，各个古老教派的神明皆以雕像呈现——希伦特、阿加什、墨托斯、亥里厄、圣特里娅——重塑与风暴的席位最为特殊：围绕大医师罗比俄斯的无面群像与托举启明星的的无名者；环形大厅的正中央是命运女神的位置。她独自立于圆桌主位方向，其余诸神的雕像环绕四周，如众星拱月。以及侍立在主神雕像旁的更多的伟大之人们——这也是万神殿名字的由来。


朝圣者们在此逐一瞻仰诸神与伟人的形象，然后继续向上——但不能再往前了。命运寝室的入口不对信徒开放。有人沉默地接受，有人面露失望。


**归乡大道**


从高塔侧后绕出，经过命运教堂群，朝圣者踏上正北大道。


这是离开的道路。正北门外是广袤的田野。朝圣者走出城门，跨入另一个世界——旧世界。田园、领主、冒险者、法师、骑士——这是希伦特城之外大部分人类的生活面貌。一些人选择留下，等待命运真正露面；更多的人就此离去，回到他们来时的生活，回到旧大陆的某个村庄或城镇，回到旧世界

## 第三幕《大学》

> **田野的尽头是大学，大学的尽头是辽阔无际的海。**
> **希伦特的“魔法”就像是空中狂风，你可以直接看见，但你的身体不够轻盈，灵魂不够通透，你不是飞鸟，你是地上被菟丝子与泥巴拽住的烂榆木**

---

**老麦田**


他们从老麦田入城——说是老麦田，但也只是“旧了”而远远称不上“老”


这是第五环北部广袤的农业区。村镇在收获季节忙碌，孩子们在村口模仿骑士与法师打闹，麦田向远方铺展，仿佛没有尽头。但麦田终究会走完。当最后一片金黄被甩在身后，脚下的路便不再是乡间土道。


**炼金工坊**


穿过农业区后进入第四环西侧——那些从旧世界各地跋涉而来的年轻法师、学徒、怀揣论文手稿的学者——他们看到的不是神的荣耀，而是自己的未来。街边排列着私人炼金工坊，这些工坊不如东侧商会那样气派，但它们更近——一个学生可以站在门口，想象自己毕业后也能租下一间。


**大医院**


沿路进入第三环。这里坐落着大医院——重塑教会的总部。


大医院的正门从不关闭。门前总有三两人等候：缠着染血绷带的冒险者、怀抱病童的农妇、从远方赶来求医的老人。接待他们的是重塑教会的学徒——这些年轻人大多同时在希伦特大学修习医术，大医院对他们而言便是教学与行医并行的附属医院，行脚医生与苦修者将会从他们之中诞生，等到他们技艺娴熟，医德远扬，追随者也会自然出现。


大医院内部有真正的大医师。赫尔曼便是其中之一。追随他的圣武士被称为“赫尔曼的苍白之手”——他们是大医师的护卫，也是血肉改造的献身者，如所有重塑圣骑一样不夸耀荣誉，只默默跟随守护对象行动。另一位大医师米瑞雅同样留在城中，但她与她的教团并不在此处。


**古城墙**


继续向东，抵达内城的古城墙。


光与线在空气中自行排列，旋转着指向不同方向：学院区向西、大书库向北、港口向东。箭头每隔几秒变换一次形态，从辉光点线渐变为清晰可辨的文字。


这个概念来自多尼雷，经过魔法体系的转译后落地生根。多尼雷人称其为“增强现实”，希伦特人沿用了这个舶来语的变形。它的基础规则并不复杂：法阵基盘规定旋转方向需使用荧光引导线标示，推斥法术的交互点则需统一设置符号标识——最初只是一些类似工程制图的辉光点线，直到有人尝试直接拼图写字——从那以后，具现标识便不再只是功能性的指引，而逐渐演变为这座城市独有的景观。


对于从旧世界传统法师教育中走出来的学生而言，这一切是翻天覆地的。他们的导师从未教过这些。魔法在他们的故乡是咒语、是符文、是师徒之间口耳相传的秘法。而在这里，魔法被嵌入了城市本身，像路灯和下水道一样理所当然。


**法学院**


穿过城门，西北方向便是正义大教堂。


但学生们不这么叫它。他们管它叫“法学院”。就如同崇死大教堂被称为“神学院”。加上枢机院与大学——整个内城——这才是广义上真正的希伦特魔法大学。他们都教授与大学内部不同的逻辑与思维，并推送着不同类型的”学生“


正义教会在这里培养的是审判官——那些身着黑袍、佩戴银臂甲、手持黑色破魔大剑的惩戒圣骑。但培养的过程远不止武艺与神术。审判官必须精通律法推理，从法典条文中推导判决，而非仅凭神启裁决是非。与之并行的另一条脉络是朴素正义的以人为本——正义女神的原则：谁受害、谁施暴、以牙还牙，以血还血，人类至上。这两条逻辑方向——律法的与人文的——共同构成了法学院的教学体系，与魔法学会的技术路线截然不同。


**枢机院**


从法学院向东，横向穿过枢机院。


朝圣者从这里竖直向上前往万神殿。而学生则在此处接受基础道德教育——命运的指导思想。这是所有进入希伦特大学的学生必须修习的课程，无论他们将来成为法师、医师、审判官还是别的什么。大学的校训刻在枢机院的入口处——“锐意进取，求真务实”。同样的一个来自多尼雷的舶来品。

**学院高塔与学院教室**


学院区没有单一的教学楼。公共教室与学院静室分布在多栋正常建筑中，任何考入或推荐入学的学生均可在此修习基础课程——魔法原理、铭文入门、元素分类学、异种族语言，诸如此类。法师塔则是更传统的大法师象征——这种建筑形式被沿用至今，每座塔属于一个学派，是导师的驻地与研究场所，不对外开放。学生须通过申请、考核与面试，被某一学派接收后，方可踏入对应的塔门与专属教室。


三位传奇大魔导师各自占据一座高塔。


赛文斯·诺尔艾恩的学派位于学院区中部。他是奥法的继承者与革新者——现代奥法的研究方向聚焦于法术的能量转化，奥法师们对幽影界隧洞穿孔效应的研究已取得重大突破。


巴卡洛夫的学派位于中上部。他曾是奥法出身的老派法师，最终走上了另一条路。魔动学派脱胎于奥法，名称取自高等精灵的魔法动力与魔法机械，专注于法术之间的嵌合与相互作用，以及与实体物体的物理互动。


斯卡蒂·斯卡哈的学派占据了最上几层。逻辑式宏魔法被誉为“魔法的未来”——最深奥，也最令人敬畏。逻辑学派的前身是研究傀儡与控灵术的法师，在该方向上积累了深厚底蕴。逻辑理论从多尼雷传入后，这些零散的分支被斯卡哈整合为一个统一的综合性学派，名称同样是多尼雷的舶来品。


传奇之下，还有更多更小的学派与分支。走廊里贴满了学术讲座的海报。这里像一座蜂巢，或一口永远在沸腾的坩埚——这是希伦特大学被称为“大学”的原因，而非仅仅是神学院、法学院、枢机院或医学院所代表的传统教育。


教会学院同样分布于学院区。自然学院主要研究古代魔法，保存着从神代到黄金时代的法术遗存与失落知识。颂乐学院专精博物百科——音乐、艺术、历史、语言，那些不依赖超凡力量的学识，皆在此处汇集。奇械学院则潜心研究多尼雷传入的科学与技术，是机械式宏魔法的理论源头之一。


**大书库与中央高塔**


学院区的南方，大书库与中央高塔并肩而立。


大书库出版《前沿术法》。创刊号的主题是斯卡蒂斯卡哈的逻辑式宏魔法，刊出后在老派法师中引发了广泛讨论。大书库本身的研究范围更为驳杂——从考古到炼金，从灵魂本质到世界树与地脉，几乎所有知识领域都在这里占有一席之地。


中央高塔的地位更高。它直接推进命运女神所支持的方向，负责指定的项目。它的名字很少出现在学生们的日常讨论中——不是因为不重要，而是因为太过遥远。那是最高研究院，魔法的未来。一个初级法师不会在食堂里谈论中央高塔正在做什么，但他们都读过《前沿术法》，都记得创刊号上那些争论，都知道那些争论正在中央高塔的某间实验室里被验证或推翻。


**内城港口**


大学路的终点是港口。


这里是希伦特的军事尖端实践地。空港与海港并立，停放着最新式的钢铁战舰与军用飞空艇。它们不再需要风帆，不再需要气囊——新式高效魔动引擎为这些战争兵器提供了动力，那是由魔动法师、奥法师、逻辑法师通力合作的产物：元素转化、魔动嵌合、逻辑自动，三门学科在每一艘战舰里同时运转。


毕业生在港口登船。他们中的一些人选择留在城内创业——在第四环租下一间炼金工坊，但更多人选择出海，前往新大陆。他们像蒲公英一样被希伦特城送出港口，散入世界。若干年后，也许会有人在某处不是希伦特城的地方挂上一块写着“锐意进取，求真务实”的牌子。而没有人再追问这句话来自哪里，又为什么挂在那里。


> 也有极少数人申请了前往多尼雷的交换名额——那是一年只有三个名额的项目，选拔标准无人知晓，回来的人也大多沉默。

# City of Sirent

---
Sirent is the name of a god. The ancient Goddess of Fate—Maria Sirent—the only true god who walks among mortals.

Sirent is the name of an empire. Since the unification of the Old Continent, it has claimed sovereignty over all the known world.

Sirent is the name of this city. The completion of the Great City was itself made the basis of the calendar.

---

## Act One: The Chronicle of City-Building

_**Conspiracy and war yield to glory and the will of god.**_

---

The site of Sirent City was chosen by Fate herself—the ruins of the ancient empire's former capital. Before the decree to build the city was issued, the Arcane Society had already studied the ruins of the Floating City for over a decade. They surveyed the broken inscription arrays, restored the lost magitech networks, and gradually pieced together those fragments that had been silent for thousands of years.

The Empire needed a new capital. The wider the conquered territory, the more it needed an unquestionable center. The Academy's achievements were therefore rapidly expanded: a city named after a god, simultaneously bearing three functions—a symbol of new power, the continuation of ancient theocracy, and the proclamation of a magical revolution.

The old Floating City was decided to be shattered into pieces and reintegrated into the new capital. The precise structures that the Academy had surveyed for over a decade were migrated as a whole from the ancient ruins, becoming the foundation and skeleton of the new city, rearranged according to the needs of the new era.

The order of conscription spread across the Old Continent. On every road in the world, streams of people appeared, migrating toward the new imperial capital. Pilgrims, refused participation in the construction of the Inner City, gathered outside its perimeter, ring after ring. They began to spontaneously build an "Outer City"—which even earlier than the Inner City took on the outline of a city. Scholars and adventurers followed the tide of pilgrims. They had heard that the Empire had established an unprecedented Arcane Society and a true university in this new city. They had also heard that the ancient Floating City ruins would not be sealed off—it would be open to all for research and exploration.

To facilitate unified planning and coordination, the Academy during this period compiled the _Requirements and Standards for Manifestation Sigils_. This document was later repeatedly revised and eventually became the world-wide _Standards and Specifications for Spellcasting_. But at the time, it had only one purpose—to enable tens of thousands of mages from different schools, speaking different terminologies, and of varying skill levels, to work together on the same magic circle.

The formal starting point of the city's construction was the year 718 of the Mechanism Calendar, which is 82 years before the Fate Calendar. The Magic Academy first completed the overall plan and the design of the magic circle foundation for the new city. This would not be a city slowly built up from the ground by stonemasons and laborers, but a city woven inch by inch by mages, starting from the core of a great magic circle. The entire foundation covered a magical construct on the scale of a Floating City; even basic construction required at least mid-level mages to participate.

When the Inner City project was halfway through, the chaos of the Outer City could no longer be ignored. The Bard College—part of the Magic University, reorganized from earlier societies and schools—sent a planning team to take over the reconstruction of the Outer City.

By the year 44 of the Fate Calendar, the reconstruction of the old Outer City was formally completed, and the plan for future expansion was simultaneously determined—the city would not stop growing, but from then on, every ring of expansion would proceed according to this plan, with ample margin reserved.

> _On the calendar: According to the old rules among the churches, the calendar rotates every thousand years. But Fate spoke personally for the birth of this city; the Mechanism Calendar ended early in the year 800, and the Fate Calendar began. The year the Inner City was completed was set as year zero of the Fate Calendar, as the new basis for the calendar._

When the construction of Sirent City began, the Empire was on a trajectory of total war. The conquest plan radiated outward from the Old Continent; the army, logistics, administration, magical industry—the entire state machine revolved around war, and this state had lasted for a considerable time. The order to build the city changed all of this.

Fate personally chose the site for the new city, meaning the will of the theocracy once again issued a decree; this time she directly intervened in the Empire's expansion process. A large number of high-level mages originally intended for the front lines were reassigned to the chosen site to participate in construction. The focus of the Arcane Society also shifted from war magic and magical industry to urban planning and the reconstruction of the great magic circle.

For an empire at war, building an entirely new capital was itself a massive reallocation of resources. Artisans, scholars, mages, materials—forces that could have been thrown into the battlefield—were transferred in batches in the same direction. The pace of conquest thus slowed.

> They say I no longer have to go to the front lines. I should feel fortunate. I've had enough of the rainforest's damp heat and those wood beasts. Though I don't know what I'll do once back home, may I at least pass these two months at sea in peace.

No one knows why Fate made this decision at this point in time. She never voluntarily explains her intentions; her commands are simply issued, and then carried out. The Empire is her creation; she forged this nation, and therefore at any time has the right to redefine its direction. The Emperor—the nominal supreme ruler—played the role of executor in this matter of city-building. His authority came from military command, and when a god spoke directly, the military leader receded to his original position.

For those generals advancing on the front lines, for those noble families who had already invested enormous resources in the conquest plan, this shift was not without resistance. The old rulers of regions about to be conquered were also hesitant; the process of surrender slowed because of the signal sent by the city's construction, or even fell into greater chaos. But the Goddess of Fate is not remote. She is the god who once again cares for "humans," the one who descended in the Black Iron Age and ended the thousand-year war. Quite a few of the Empire's founders had once walked with her. Questioning a legend is one thing; questioning a real god standing before you is another. What the opponents did was more about private schemes between factions and hidden games—the efforts of a group of calculating people trying to preserve their positions amid the turning of an era.

Thus, the Empire's war machine did not stop, but its center of gravity shifted. Conquest continued, but resources, attention, and symbolic meaning all tilted toward the city being built in the name of god. The various powers of the Old Continent noticed this. The unconquered regions noticed this. The enemies still resisting also noticed this.

The enemies gained breathing room, and the time of world unification was postponed once again. But Fate has never erred, and Sirent is utterly loyal to her.

### Inner City

> **The Empire attempts to establish a new order in a broken world, but the games among races, churches, and old and new powers grow ever more complex. Contact with otherworldly civilizations such as "Tonile" has brought entirely new technologies, ideas, and social models, delivering a massive shock to Sirent's traditions.**

The Inner City is circular, with the Central Tower as the control center and focal point of the city-wide magic circle. Six main roads radiate from the Central Tower, leading respectively to six city gates. Due south is the Pilgrim's Avenue, passing through the Ceremonial Plaza, from the South Gate directly to the Tower of Fate, and finally to the Pantheon. Due north is the Departure Road, which splits into two paths behind the side of the Grand Cathedral, bypasses the church complex, and converges into a single avenue leading to the North Gate. Northeast connects to the Inner City Harbor, including the seaport and the northern Air Harbor Tower, now for military and academy use only. Southeast connects to the Outer City Harbor; the two are linked but separated by military isolation, and can be unified when necessary. Northwest passes through the Grand Cathedral of Justice, southwest connects to the Church of Death's faithful district, and due west leads to the Academy District—these three roads all connect to the Outer City after leaving the Inner City.

The Inner City also has an elevated ring road encircling the Central Tower, passing in turn through the Tower of Fate, the Cardinalate, the Grand Cathedral of Justice, the Church of Death, the Great Library, the Academy Tower, and the Air Harbor Tower. Where the ring road intersects with the six radiating main roads from the Central Tower, there are roundabout hubs where roads can be switched.

The ground-level areas of the Inner City are distributed by direction. The Central Tower is maintained by the Arcane Society and the University of Sirent. To its east is the Tower of Fate, the site of the Church of Fate's complex, the Cardinalate, and the Pantheon, and the terminus of the Pilgrim's Avenue. Northwest of the Tower of Fate is the Grand Cathedral of Justice, pierced by the northwest main road. Southwest is the Church of Death's district, including the church complex and the faithful district. The large area from south to northeast is the Academy District, including the Great Library, the Academy castle complex, the mage tower cluster, the Academy Council, and the Academy Tower; the Central Tower is also within its management scope, now also including the northeast coastal Inner City Harbor and Inner City Air Harbor.

The Inner City still retains the above-ground walls of the ancient Floating City. The residential planning is also still in use, but currently the buildings in the Inner City that still serve only residential functions are mainly inns, hotels, and a few villa-style detached antique residences, for visiting scholars, diplomatic envoys, and high-ranking clergy.

The infrastructure skeleton of the Inner City is also not entirely newly built. The drainage network, main road configuration, and energy supply network of the old Floating City have been used to this day—the current six-spoke structure of the Central Tower passage originates from this. The floating rune array has also been fully repaired and preserved. In theory, the Inner City possesses the ability to float as a whole.

The underground part of the Inner City is roughly divided into two layers. By region: beneath the Church of Death complex is the Great Underground Tomb, under church jurisdiction; beneath the Great Library is the Great Underground Library, larger than the above-ground portion; beneath the Academy District are the Academy Quiet Rooms and Great Lecture Halls, used to create simulated environments such as the Floating Sea and the Dark Regions. These three basically divide the underground space into thirds, with the central control group located in the very center—directly beneath the Central Tower, the core control hub of the city-wide magic circle. The deeper layer is the support layer for city functions, arranged with layer upon layer of magic circles and power furnace cores, in the form of giant pillars and magic circle discs, constantly rotating and mechanically assembling, driving the surface city's various functions according to preset programs, such as magical energy lamps, Manifestation Sigils, dynamic landscapes, rail transit, etc.

In the early planning of the Inner City, positions were reserved for the various ancient churches according to covenant. But as the city developed, some churches, due to not having a practical need for a permanent presence in the Inner City, gradually changed the function of their reserved areas, most merging into the University: the Church of Fate occupied the core position in the Inner City, namely the Tower of Fate and the Cardinalate. The Church of Justice retained the Grand Cathedral of Justice. The Church of Death retained the church complex, the cemetery complex, and the Great Underground Tomb. The Church of Magic is the University itself; they are also the maintainers of the entire city's magic circle. The Church of Song merged into the University, transforming into the Bard College. The Church of Reshaping retained a college and council seat within the University; its headquarters—the Great Hospital that undertakes medical functions—moved to the Outer City. The Church of Storms was originally allotted the Inner City Harbor area, then moved to the Outer City Harbor. The Church of Nature retained a college and council seat within the University; its personnel are mostly located in the wilderness.

### Outer City

> **Integration, exploration, conflict, and identity crisis—the reconstruction of order and the explosive expansion of the cognitive frontier. This is a new chapter full of infinite possibility and infinite danger.**

**Third Ring**

The first to arrive was an architectural sect from the Church of Death, later called the "City-Building School." They were known for their marble and theological aesthetics, traditional builders of grand cathedrals. This sect had already participated in the planning stage of Sirent City, laying the initial planning logic and aesthetic foundation for the city. After negotiation, the sect's mages and priests remained in the Inner City to participate in magical infrastructure, while the traditional artisans remained outside the city. These artisans participated in the maintenance of the Ancient City Wall and planned the earliest residential areas along the sections where the Inner City roads extended outward, while building several churches to receive the pilgrims arriving later. These settlements later developed into the prototype of the Outer City.

As news of the city's construction spread, more pilgrims arrived one after another, with the majority being followers of the Church of Fate, among them also elves from afar. Unlike other races, the elves retained a certain tendency to congregate, forming a relatively concentrated block on the northwest side of the Third Ring—not closed, but elven shops and language were more common there. Other races mixed normally. Then came engineering contractors and labor unions, bringing more labor and material circulation. The population of the Third Ring swelled rapidly during this period.

After the Bard College took over the reconstruction of the Outer City, the Third Ring was included in the first batch of reorganization. The reconstruction retained the road skeleton planned by the early artisans, widened it, and incorporated it into the ring road system—the name "Ring" derives from this. Churches and plazas were retained as landmarks. The main new regulation was a height limit for buildings on the side facing the Ancient City Wall, not to exceed the top of the wall. The architectural appearance was mainly red brick and brown sloping tiles, generally five to six stories, with neat and continuous street facades. Apartments were the most common form of housing. The Great Hospital of the Church of Reshaping moved here.

Today the Third Ring is the main residential area of the Outer City. Its shape is crescent-like, surrounding the land boundary of the Inner City, with the seaside side left vacant as the harbor district. The three main roads exiting the Inner City to the northwest, southwest, and due west converge here, leading respectively toward the Grand Cathedral of Justice, the Church of Death district, and the Academy District. Along the main roads are several early churches, whose attached small plazas are daily gathering places for local residents. Since students of the University of Sirent live directly in the Inner City, the Third Ring does not serve as student housing.

**Fourth Ring**

The Fourth Ring is larger than the Third Ring, divided by function into multiple areas.

With the Central Avenue extending from the Inner City's due-south road as the boundary, the eastern seaside area is the Harbor District. This includes the old port and the new comprehensive port. The Church of Storms moved its church and observatory here from the Inner City; the Church of the Deep's ocean guild and small chapel also settled here—the latter has branches in ports all over the world, and Sirent City is naturally no exception. The Harbor District also includes a dam built to reinforce the coast and prevent damp, as well as a large civil air harbor. Several Air Harbor Towers and their fan platforms cover the area above the port; some complained that this blocked the sunlight, but acknowledged that it also blocked the wind and waves.

West of the Central Avenue is the Workshop District and the Chamber of Commerce headquarters. The Adventurers' Guild established its headquarters here. The Golden Dragon Bank, Lettuce Vine, and Oddities & Oddities and other trading houses all operate here, along with many private workshops. New ideas from the University are put into practice here, while traditional needs are mass-produced on assembly lines.

The Fourth Ring is the economic center of Sirent City—and the economic center of the world. Because building height is unrestricted, trading houses and workshops compete to build higher buildings, to overlook the harbor or gaze at the tower cluster of the Inner City. Manifestation Sigils are widely used on building facades—the signs of trading houses hover on the building surfaces in the form of light and color, with prices and announcements refreshed in real time; at night the entire district is illuminated by magical energy fluorescence as if it were day. This spectacle is unique in the world of Sirent: factory output, trading house orders, port throughput—these tangible things support the artificial dense forest of the Fourth Ring. For observers from Tonile, there is a familiar sense of nostalgia; for locals of Sirent, this is the face of the "new era" after reform.

**Fifth Ring**

The Fifth Ring is the outermost ring of the Outer City, still continuously expanding. However, it is vaguely divided into two distinctly different areas, north and south.

The north is vast farmland. These farmlands existed before the city was built; the farmers who had cultivated them for generations were uniformly relocated and settled in planned areas far from the Inner City. They still farm, and perhaps in their leisure they gaze at the distant, ever-growing giant city—a world they do not understand. Or perhaps their children will, years later, step into the place their ancestors once lived as students. This is the silent rift between the new Sirent City and the old world.

The south is a massive industrial zone. Unlike the precise private alchemical workshops of the Fourth Ring, its functions are shipyards, golem foundries, and large-scale factory production facilities. The world's most advanced airships and engineering golems—whether physical or conceptual—complete their final assembly here, then ship out to all parts of the world.

Cheaper and higher-quality land than the Fourth Ring attracts ambitious people to continue expanding outward. The University of Sirent is the center of this city, and also the world's largest producer of ambitious people. Those students, with novel ideas and the usual action of the extraordinary, view the Fifth Ring as a testing ground for ideas. Higher towers rise here, and farther boundaries are pushed outward from here.

## Act Two: The Pilgrims

> **Pilgrims gather from all over the world, just to see the true god lost for a thousand years.**  
> **As long as she stands there, there will be no more war in the world.**

---

**Old Shipyard**

They enter the city from the industrial zone in the south of the Fifth Ring.

The first thing that comes into view is the skeleton of an airship in midair, its huge curved ribs surrounded by a colony of grey-iron ants, not yet covered with skin, revealing the internal keel structure. On the ground are arranged half-finished industrial golems, their joints still open, waiting for the next step of assembly. There is no vegetation here—the sandy ground raises fine dust in dry weather, falling on the pilgrims' robes and bare feet. The workers labor in this dust. They themselves are also believers. Some stop their work and hand over water skins and dry rations; others just stand silently under the steel eaves, watching—they remember how it was when they first walked here.

**Tower Cluster**

After passing through the industrial zone, they enter the Fourth Ring. The pilgrims have never seen such a bustling street market. The most prosperous city-states of the southern kingdoms, the golden capital of the Old Sahra Empire—none can compare to this place. Some even recall the descriptions of the Elven Dynasty in ancient books—the Silver Age that built the Floating City, the legendary era when mortals were closest to god—even that era might not match the scene before them. The pilgrims attribute this to god. They notice the names of some trading houses along the street—perhaps they have seen the same names in their homeland—but here, the same names hang on storefronts several times higher, becoming unattainable.

**Grand Chapel**

Continuing north along the Pilgrim's Avenue, they enter the Third Ring. The first Church of Death appears by the roadside. This is the first grand cathedral the pilgrims see in Sirent City. On either side of the main entrance are gargoyles. On the left is Madeleine's _The Sorrows of the Mother_, on the right is Buonarotti's _The New Chronicle of City-Building_.

The subject of _The Sorrows of the Mother_ is fixed—it depicts the story of the suffering of Agash, Goddess of Life, one of the oldest narratives of the orthodox church. Traditionally, _The Sorrows of the Mother_ chooses a female saint who ended her life in charitable works as the bearer of its soul. _The Chronicle of City-Building_, on the other hand, takes the process of city construction as its subject, with one person selected from among the church's great sculptors to wield the hammer.

But the outer-city chapel of Sirent City did not entirely follow this convention. In _The Chronicle of City-Building_, the City-Building sect altered the subject, while in _The Sorrows of the Mother_, the mainline pushed it to the extreme.

Sirent City's Madeleine _The Sorrows of the Mother_ is considered the pinnacle of Flamboyantism. The entire statue is said to depict over three hundred figures, from every feather on every angel's wing to the thorns entwined around the sword, from the veins of olive leaves to the tendrils of grape arbors, to the surging clouds at the end of heaven—all details rendered in marble, leaving no blank space. Most striking are the three faces of the Mother: the main face weeping, the side faces sleeping and laughing—the stone was worked by the artisan with such painstaking effort that it achieved a "veiled" texture, as if truly covering her face.

This is the pursuit of Flamboyantism: to tell an epic with three hundred figures, to restore the glory of god with a thousand chisel marks.

But in the eyes of the City-Building sect, such a work does not glorify Agash, but glorifies the sculptor himself.

So they brought out Buonarotti's _The New Chronicle of City-Building_. The City-Building sect believed that the construction of Sirent City should not be framed within traditional formats, so they redefined the subject and skipped the church's internal selection, submitting the proposal directly to the Goddess of Fate. The goddess chose it.

Buonarotti was placed on the other side of the cathedral's main entrance. Its style is diametrically opposed to Madeleine on the left: the form is composed of rough geometric blocks, chisel marks exposed, the natural texture of marble fully preserved, no angels, no surging clouds, no superfluous figures. The city upheld by those pairs of faceless hands—nothing remains but the solemnity and power of the stone itself. The soul it carries is also unusual—said to be a great sculptor of the City-Building School. Buonarotti is extremely quiet, standing silently at the entrance year-round, rarely moving.

As for Madeleine herself—you are not likely to find her at the cathedral entrance. She is an active person, daily visiting the parish, teaching theology classes, talking with children. The magnificent epic of over three hundred figures accompanies a kindly elder, who directly implements the knowledge of the ancients.

**Ancient City Wall**

The pilgrims rest here, then continue north, arriving at the Ancient City Wall of the Inner City.

The stone of the wall has a marble-like quality, but its surface is covered with decorations of an entirely different style—leaf veins, curves imitating petals, entwined vine patterns—these carvings do not belong to the Church of Death, nor to humans. They come from a more ancient era, relics of the Elven Dynasty of the Silver Age. The "ancient" that the City-Building School seeks to emulate.

After the fall of the Elven Dynasty, the world entered the Black Iron Age. Kingdoms, religions, and races fell into a long war. Humans, elves, dwarves, and demihumans—no one could end this melee. Then the Goddess of Fate descended. She favored Sirent—a human kingdom that was then unremarkable. Under her protection, Sirent rose rapidly.

**Ceremonial Plaza**

Passing through the South Gate, following the avenue north, bypassing the ring island of the Central Tower, one arrives at the Ceremonial Plaza in front of the Cardinalate.

There are no statues in the plaza; the space is left open for all to witness. The Goddess of Fate ended the disputes among the sects, and then led Sirent to end the civil war among the humanoid races. After that came the conquest of the alien races. And when the Demon Realm fell and the Demon King descended, Fate blessed the heroes—each hero, in this very place, in this plaza, received the blessing, and then departed for the fated battle with the Demon King.

**Tower of Fate**

North of the plaza is the Tower of Fate. The pilgrims enter the tower, first passing through the Cardinalate.

The Church of Fate is the other half of the Empire's administrative skeleton. In remote villages, the words of the resident priest often carry more weight than the lord's orders; in towns, the city bishop corresponds to each level of secular administration, as if there were another parallel system of governance alongside the village chief and mayor. The Cardinalate is the hub of this system, directly receiving orders from the Emperor, governing the dispatch and execution of military, political, and economic affairs.

Beyond the Church of Fate, there are many churches undertaking specific social functions. The Church of Death manages cemeteries and funerals—the tombs beneath each church, and the undertakers and sculptors throughout the world. The Church of Reshaping is based on medicine; its Great Hospital is located in the Third Ring, with pain and compassion as the foundation of its doctrine.

**Pantheon**

Continuing upward along the tower, one enters the Pantheon.

The Pantheon is a circular hall with a round table at its center. Around the round table, the gods of the various ancient sects are presented as statues—Sirent, Agash, Morthos[no yet], Helios[no yet], Sentria[no yet]—the seats of Reshaping and Storms are the most special: the faceless group around the Great Physician Robius and the nameless one holding the morning star; at the very center of the circular hall is the position of the Goddess of Fate. She stands alone in the direction of the head of the round table, with the statues of the other gods surrounding her like stars around the moon. And the many great ones standing beside the statue of the chief god—this is also the origin of the name Pantheon.

The pilgrims here gaze upon the images of the gods and great ones one by one, then continue upward—but they can go no further. The entrance to Fate's bedchamber is not open to believers. Some accept in silence, some show disappointment.

**Homeward Avenue**

Emerging from behind the side of the tower, passing through the Church of Fate complex, the pilgrims step onto the due-north avenue.

This is the road of departure. Outside the North Gate is vast farmland. The pilgrims walk out of the city gate and step into another world—the old world. Fields, lords, adventurers, mages, knights—this is the life of most humans outside Sirent City. Some choose to stay, waiting for Fate to truly appear; more leave, returning to the life they came from, to a village or town somewhere on the Old Continent, back to the old world.

## Act Three: The University

> **At the end of the fields is the University; at the end of the University is the vast, boundless sea.**  
> **Sirent's "magic" is like a wild wind in the air—you can see it directly, but your body is not light enough, your soul not transparent enough. You are not a bird; you are rotten elm wood dragged down by dodder and mud.**

---

**Old Wheat Fields**

They enter the city from the Old Wheat Fields—though called Old Wheat Fields, it is only "old" and far from "ancient."

This is the vast agricultural area in the north of the Fifth Ring. Villages are busy in harvest season; children play at knights and mages at the village entrance; wheat fields stretch into the distance as if without end. But the wheat fields eventually end. When the last patch of gold is thrown behind, the road underfoot is no longer a country dirt path.

**Alchemical Workshops**

After passing through the agricultural area, they enter the western side of the Fourth Ring—those young mages, apprentices, and scholars clutching thesis manuscripts who have trekked from all over the old world—what they see is not the glory of god, but their own future. Private alchemical workshops line the street; these workshops are not as grand as the trading houses on the east side, but they are closer—a student can stand at the door and imagine renting one after graduation.

**Great Hospital**

Following the road into the Third Ring. Here stands the Great Hospital—the headquarters of the Church of Reshaping.

The main entrance of the Great Hospital is never closed. There are always two or three people waiting at the door: adventurers with blood-stained bandages, farm women holding sick children, old people who have come from afar seeking medical help. They are received by apprentices of the Church of Reshaping—these young people mostly also study medicine at the University of Sirent; the Great Hospital is to them an affiliated hospital where teaching and practice run in parallel. Itinerant doctors and ascetics will be born from among them; when their skills are proficient and their medical ethics widely known, followers will naturally appear.

Inside the Great Hospital are true Great Physicians. Hermann is one of them. The paladins who follow him are called "Hermann's Pale Hand"—they are the guards of the Great Physician, and also devotees of flesh modification; like all Reshaping Paladins, they do not boast of honor, but silently follow the one they guard. Another Great Physician, Mireya, also remains in the city, but she and her order are not here.

**Ancient City Wall**

Continuing east, they arrive at the Ancient City Wall of the Inner City.

Light and lines arrange themselves in the air, rotating to point in different directions: Academy District to the west, Great Library to the north, Harbor to the east. The arrows change form every few seconds, gradually shifting from glowing dotted lines to clearly legible text.

This concept comes from Tonile, and after translation through the magical system, it took root. The people of Tonile call it "augmented reality"; the people of Sirent adopted a variation of this borrowed term. Its basic rules are not complex: the magic circle base plate stipulates that rotation directions must be indicated with fluorescent guide lines, and the interaction points of repulsion spells must be uniformly marked with symbols—initially just some glowing dotted lines like engineering drawings, until someone tried to directly arrange them into words—from then on, Manifestation Sigils were no longer merely functional guides, but gradually evolved into a landscape unique to this city.

For students coming from traditional mage education in the old world, all of this is earth-shaking. Their mentors never taught these things. Magic in their homelands was spells, runes, secret arts passed down orally between master and apprentice. But here, magic is embedded in the city itself, as a matter of course as street lamps and sewers.

**College of Law**

Passing through the city gate, to the northwest is the Grand Cathedral of Justice.

But the students do not call it that. They call it the "College of Law." Just as the Church of Death Cathedral is called the "College of Divinity." Together with the Cardinalate and the University—the entire Inner City—this is the true University of Sirent Magic in the broad sense. They all teach different logic and thinking from within the University, and push different types of "students."

The Church of Justice trains inquisitors here—those punitive paladins in black robes, silver arm guards, and black demon-breaking greatswords. But the training process goes far beyond martial arts and divine arts. Inquisitors must be proficient in legal reasoning, deriving judgments from legal texts, rather than deciding right and wrong solely by divine revelation. Running parallel is another thread of plain justice based on people—the principle of the Goddess of Justice: whoever is harmed, whoever harms, an eye for an eye, blood for blood, humanity supreme. These two logical directions—legal and humanistic—together constitute the teaching system of the College of Law, entirely different from the technical route of the Arcane Society.

**Cardinalate**

From the College of Law eastward, crossing through the Cardinalate.

Pilgrims go vertically upward from here to the Pantheon. Students, on the other hand, receive basic moral education here—the guiding thought of Fate. This is a required course for all students entering the University of Sirent, regardless of whether they will become mages, physicians, inquisitors, or something else. The university motto is carved at the entrance of the Cardinalate—"Strive Forward, Seek Truth and Be Practical." Another borrowed product from Tonile.

**Academy Tower and Academy Classrooms**

The Academy District has no single teaching building. Public classrooms and Academy Quiet Rooms are distributed among several normal buildings; any student admitted by examination or recommendation can study basic courses here—magic principles, introduction to inscriptions, elemental taxonomy, alien languages, and so on. Mage towers are a more traditional symbol of the great mage—this architectural form is still in use today; each tower belongs to a school, is the residence and research place of a mentor, and is not open to the public. Students must apply, pass examination and interview, and be accepted by a school before they can step into the corresponding tower gate and exclusive classroom.

Three legendary Archmages each occupy a tower.

Sevens Nolaein[no yet]'s school is located in the middle of the Academy District. He is the inheritor and innovator of Arcana—modern Arcana research focuses on the energy conversion of spells; Arcana mages have made major breakthroughs in the study of the tunnel perforation effect of the Shadow Realm.

Bakarov[no yet]'s school is located in the upper middle. He was an old-school mage of Arcana origin, who ultimately took another path. The Magimotive School emerged from Arcana; its name is taken from the magical power and magical machinery of the High Elves, focusing on the interlocking and interaction between spells, and physical interaction with entities.

Skadi Skaha[no yet]'s school occupies the top few floors. Logic-Style Macro-Magic is hailed as "the future of magic"—the most profound, and also the most awe-inspiring. The Logic School's predecessor was mages studying puppets and soul-control arts, accumulating deep foundations in that direction. After logic theory was introduced from Tonile, these scattered branches were integrated by Skaha into a unified comprehensive school; the name is likewise a borrowed product from Tonile.

Below the legends, there are many smaller schools and branches. Corridors are plastered with posters for academic lectures. It is like a beehive, or a cauldron forever boiling—this is why the University of Sirent is called a "university," rather than merely the traditional education represented by the College of Divinity, College of Law, Cardinalate, or Medical College.

Church colleges are also distributed in the Academy District. The College of Nature mainly studies ancient magic, preserving spell relics and lost knowledge from the Age of Gods to the Golden Age. The College of Song specializes in encyclopedic knowledge—music, art, history, language, those learnings that do not rely on extraordinary power, all gathered here. The College of Mechanisms devotes itself to studying the science and technology introduced from Tonile, and is one of the theoretical sources of mechanical macro-magic.

**Great Library and Central Tower**

In the south of the Academy District, the Great Library and the Central Tower stand side by side.

The Great Library publishes _Frontier Spellcraft_. The theme of the inaugural issue was Skadi Skaha's Logic-Style Macro-Magic, which after publication sparked widespread discussion among old-school mages. The Great Library's own research scope is more diverse—from archaeology to alchemy, from the nature of the soul to the World Tree and ley lines, almost all fields of knowledge have a place here.

The Central Tower holds a higher position. It directly advances the direction supported by the Goddess of Fate, and is responsible for designated projects. Its name rarely appears in students' daily discussions—not because it is unimportant, but because it is too distant. That is the Supreme Research Institute, the future of magic. A junior mage will not talk about what the Central Tower is doing in the cafeteria, but they have all read _Frontier Spellcraft_, they all remember those debates in the inaugural issue, and they all know those debates are being verified or overturned in some laboratory in the Central Tower.

**Inner City Harbor**

The end of University Road is the harbor.

This is Sirent's cutting-edge military practice ground. Air harbor and seaport stand side by side, berthing the latest steel warships and military airships. They no longer need sails, no longer need gasbags—new high-efficiency magimotive engines provide power for these weapons of war; they are the product of the joint efforts of Magimotive mages, Arcana mages, and Logic mages: elemental conversion, magimotive interlocking, logic automation—three disciplines operating simultaneously in every warship.

Graduates board ships at the harbor. Some of them choose to stay in the city and start businesses—renting an alchemical workshop in the Fourth Ring—but more choose to go to sea, heading for the New Continent. They are sent out from the harbor of Sirent City like dandelions, scattering into the world. Years later, perhaps someone will hang a sign reading "Strive Forward, Seek Truth and Be Practical" somewhere that is not Sirent City. And no one will ask again where this sentence came from, or why it is hung there.

> There are also a very few who apply for the exchange quota to Tonile—a program with only three slots per year, selection criteria unknown, and most of those who return remain silent.
