克隆人是多尼雷社会中数量稀少却至关重要的特殊群体。他们并非自然繁衍的产物，而是分别克隆自原生人基因范本。  
克隆人的必要性要追溯至那场改写文明的世界大战之前——旧时代的诸多核心科技被施加了严苛的“基因锁”。唯有通过这些特定遗传密码验证的个体，才有权限接触并操作那些维系社会运行的“古代”机器与设施。   
因此，克隆人被系统地分散安置于社会各关键节点，以确保那些战前遗产得以持续运转，同时也保证智械族群的忠诚。   
克隆人除必要的血肉组织外，他们对自身躯体进行了远超常规的机械化改造，其体内集成了各式威力惊人的内置义体系统与飞升武装。相较于仿生人，他们不仅拥有更强的身体强度，更独享这些高精尖武器的核心操作权限。

Clones are a rare yet crucial special group within Tonile society. They are not the product of natural reproduction, but are cloned separately from the genetic templates of Originals.  
The necessity of Clones dates back to before the world war that rewrote civilization—many core technologies of the old era were subjected to strict "genetic locks." Only individuals verified through these specific genetic codes have the authority to access and operate the "ancient" machines and facilities that keep society running.  
Therefore, Clones are systematically dispersed and stationed at key nodes throughout society, ensuring that those pre-war legacies continue to operate, while also guaranteeing the loyalty of the synthetic populace.  
Apart from the necessary flesh and blood tissue, Clones have subjected their bodies to mechanized modifications far beyond the norm. Their bodies integrate various built-in prosthetic systems and ascension armaments of astonishing power. Compared to Bioroids, they not only possess greater physical strength, but also exclusively hold core operational access to these advanced weapons.
