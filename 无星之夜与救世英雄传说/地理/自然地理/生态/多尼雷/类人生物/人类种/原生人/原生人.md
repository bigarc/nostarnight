原生人是多尼雷数量最为稀少、受到最严密保护的群体。具体数量与位置被严格监密。他们是历经世界大战后幸存下来，基因未受污染的珍贵原生人类。  
因此，他们被严格禁止使用任何未经安全验证，或可能对基因序列产生影响的药物与改造技术。 他们的肉体保持着原始的状态，与希伦特的凡人类似，显得脆弱而有限。正是这种脆弱，与他们所代表的文明形成了极具张力的反差——  
令人难以想象，如此辉煌的文明竟由这般平凡的躯体所缔造，而又是怎样一场毁天灭地的大战，最终令其崩塌。

Originals are the rarest and most closely guarded group in Tonile. Their exact numbers and locations are strictly classified. They are the precious original humans who survived the world war with uncontaminated genes.  
Therefore, they are strictly forbidden from using any drugs or modification technologies that have not been safety-verified or that might affect their genetic sequence. Their bodies remain in a primordial state; similar to the mortals of Sirent, they appear fragile and limited. It is precisely this fragility that forms a tension-filled contrast with the civilization they represent—  
It is hard to imagine that such a glorious civilization was created by such ordinary bodies, and what kind of world-shattering war ultimately caused its collapse.
