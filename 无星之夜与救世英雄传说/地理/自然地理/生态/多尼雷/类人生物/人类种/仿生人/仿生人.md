多尼雷社会的主体由仿生人构成。尽管他们诞生于生产线与培养舱，却在每一个层面都与自然生命无异：  
他们经历完整的教育，在社会化抚养中建立深厚的情感纽带，将与同一抚养园中成长的同伴视为家人，并沿着学习、工作、衰老的生命轨迹走过一生。他们完全继承了多尼雷的文明、文化、意识形态乃至那份特有的傲慢气质。  
在生理层面，仿生人的身躯由高度优化的人造血液与高效器官驱动，不仅外观与原生人类毫无二致，身体素质更实现了跨越式增强。若以希伦特的标准衡量，几乎每一位仿生人都具备高级战士级别的体能。  
在法律与社会的认知中，他们无疑是完整的公民——不是“坚定地”认为自身是”人“，而是默认为出生方式不同的人类亚种。

Tonile society is primarily composed of Bioroids. Although they are born from production lines and incubation chambers, they are no different from natural life in every aspect:  
They undergo a complete education, form deep emotional bonds through socialized upbringing, regard those who grew up in the same rearing garden as family, and live out their lives along the trajectory of study, work, and aging. They have fully inherited Tonile's civilization, culture, ideology, and even its distinctive haughty demeanor.  
On the physiological level, the bodies of Bioroids are driven by highly optimized artificial blood and high-efficiency organs. Not only is their appearance indistinguishable from that of Originals, but their physical capabilities have achieved leapfrog enhancement. Measured by Sirent's standards, nearly every Bioroid possesses the physical fitness of an advanced warrior.  
In legal and social perception, they are undoubtedly full citizens—not "firmly" convinced that they are "human," but rather regarded as a human subspecies with a different method of birth.
