**The Dragon of Wailing and Strife**

十二条枷锁集于其身  
只剩不死的九头之龙  
正等待着赫拉克勒斯  
盼他战胜自身的死亡

Twelve shackles bound upon its body,  
Leaving only the immortal nine-headed dragon.  
It awaits Heracles,  
Hoping he will overcome his own death.

---

枷锁束缚了白龙之形，失去一切，才得以存在。

如果十二试炼一一被拆解，它获得天生的权柄，那它也走向了死亡。

它是不死的海德拉，也是刀枪不入的涅墨亚。

它是奔跑的牝鹿，是愤怒的野猪，是污秽的畜圈，是湖中的怪鸟，是失魂的疯牛，是食人的恶马

它听命于希波吕忒，为革律翁所有，它代表着金苹果，被永缚于冥府之底

它是赫拉克勒斯本身，倒于无法战胜命运的第十三试炼

The shackles bound the form of the White Dragon; only by losing everything could it exist.

If the twelve labors are dismantled one by one, and it gains its innate authority, then it also moves toward death.

It is the immortal Hydra, and also the invulnerable Nemean Lion.

It is the running Ceryneian Hind, the wrathful Erymanthian Boar, the filthy Augean Stables, the monstrous birds of the lake, the soul-lost Cretan Bull, the man-eating Mares of Diomedes.

It obeys Hippolyta, is owned by Geryon, it represents the Golden Apples, eternally bound at the depths of Hades

It is Heracles himself, fallen in the Thirteenth Labor that cannot overcome fate.
