# 异常生命体
**Aberrant Lifeforms**

异常生命体是那些“异常且不自然”的人造生物，来自无底线的试验。是没有生物的形、没有生命的魂、全凭肉体逻辑的诡异造物。  
在多尼雷的“神”面前，强大者也仅仅是凡人，他们创造了神的灵魂，还要创造神的肉体。

Aberrant Lifeforms are those "aberrant and unnatural" artificial creatures, born from unscrupulous experiments. They possess no biological form and no living soul—grotesque creations governed entirely by the logic of flesh.  
Before Tonile's "god," even the powerful are merely mortals. They created the soul of a god, and now they seek to create its flesh.
