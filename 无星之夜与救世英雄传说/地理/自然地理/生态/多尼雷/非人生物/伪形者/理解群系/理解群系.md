# 理解群系  
**Binah Biome**

理解（Binah）群系——「生态接口」。伪形者中为多尼雷准备的工具、生物器具——是别样的自然亲和。对应卡巴拉生命树第三质点，左柱之首，接纳、孕育、赋予形式。理解群系不是生态中的角色，而是接口——多尼雷人触碰、感知、操纵伪生态圈的中介层。它们既非表演者，亦非维持者，而是“观看方式”。

The **Binah Biome** — "Interfield Interaction." Among the Pseudomorphs, they are tools and biological instruments prepared for the people of Tonile—a different kind of natural affinity. It corresponds to Binah, the third sephirah of the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, the head of the Left Pillar, representing reception, gestation, and the giving of form. The Binah Biome is not a role within the ecology, but an interface—the mediating layer through which the people of Tonile touch, perceive, and manipulate the pseudo-ecosphere. They are neither performers nor maintainers, but a "way of seeing."
