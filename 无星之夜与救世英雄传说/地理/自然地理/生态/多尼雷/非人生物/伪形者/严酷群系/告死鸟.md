**Death-Herald**

每一个伪形者的每一块血肉中都根植着告死鸟子程序，当"死线"（严酷的唤醒界限）被触发，伪形者便会脱胎为战斗形态——告死鸟，如同寄生蛾一般从原生物的每一根肌腱中羽化，只余晶莹的透明囊壳。 告死鸟有多种形态，从血肉肌腱中羽化的告死鸟群，以原生物脊柱骨骼为核心蜕生的报丧女妖，以及只有以巨兽为基才能成型的白化巢穴树。

Rooted in every piece of flesh of every Pseudomorph is the Death-Herald subroutine. When the "Death Line" (the Gevurah awakening threshold) is triggered, the Pseudomorph transforms into a combat form—the Death-Herald—which, like a parasitic moth, ecloses from every tendon of the original organism, leaving behind only a crystalline, transparent husk.

The Death-Herald has multiple forms: the Death-Herald swarm that ecloses from flesh and tendons; the Banshee, which emerges from the original creature's spinal skeleton as its core; and the Albino Nest Tree, which can only take shape when based on a giant beast.
