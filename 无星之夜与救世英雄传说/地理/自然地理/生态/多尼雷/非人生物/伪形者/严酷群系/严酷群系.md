# 严酷群系  
**Gevurah Biome**

严酷（Gevurah）群系。多尼雷的「自然之怒」。对应卡巴拉生命树第五质点，左柱，象征收缩、裁断与边界。严酷群系是生命蓝图的战争状态造物，平时休眠，仅在生态圈失平衡难以维持时被唤醒。

The Gevurah Biome (Severity Biome). Tonile's "Wrath of Nature." It corresponds to Gevurah, the fifth sephirah of the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, on the Left Pillar, symbolizing contraction, judgment, and boundaries. The Gevurah Biome is a war-state creation of the Life Blueprint, normally dormant, awakened only when the biosphere falls out of balance and becomes difficult to sustain.
