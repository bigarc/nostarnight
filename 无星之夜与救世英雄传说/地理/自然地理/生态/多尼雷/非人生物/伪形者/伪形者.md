# 伪形者  
**Pseudomorphs**

在多尼雷，自然生命完全灭绝。如今生态穹顶内或城外废土的一切“自然生物”，皆是人造物——它们被统称为“伪形者”。   
它们是已逝生态的精密仿品。由于太阳远离，地脉寂静无声，驱动这群仿生兽活动的，全然是多尼雷庞大的人造能源网络。  
尽管外观千差万别——温顺的食草兽、凶猛的捕食者，甚至复原的超级生物——其本质却与流水线上的智械无异。它们由相同的生物质基材“打印”而成，共享着完全一致的底层构造。   
而且残酷的是——无论形态如何，它们的肉质品尝起来，味道全部和仿生人相同。  （当然我没真吃过，本地仿生人饭桌上讲的）  

In Tonile, natural life has gone completely extinct. Today, all "natural organisms" within the ecological domes or in the wasteland outside the cities are artificial—collectively known as "Pseudomorphs."  
They are precise facsimiles of a vanished ecology. Because the sun is distant and the ley lines are silent, what drives these bionic beasts is entirely Tonile's vast artificial energy network.  
Although their appearances vary wildly—gentle herbivores, ferocious predators, even restored super-organisms—their essence is no different from the synthetic intelligences on the assembly line. They are "printed" from the same biomass substrate, sharing an entirely identical underlying structure.  
And cruelly—regardless of form, their flesh tastes exactly the same as Bioroids. (Of course, I've never actually eaten it; that's just what local Bioroids say at the dinner table.)
