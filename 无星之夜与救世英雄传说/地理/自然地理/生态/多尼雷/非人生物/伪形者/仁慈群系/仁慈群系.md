# 仁慈群系  
**Chesed Biome**

仁慈（Chesed）群系——「修复者」。伪形者中的自然修复者，与严酷群系互为镜像。对应卡巴拉生命树第四质点，右柱之首，扩张与包容之力。严酷制止破坏，仁慈则在破坏停止后现身——以其群系物种生命的循环修补废墟、重植残枝、缝合裂痕。

The **Chesed Biome** — "The Restorers." Among the Pseudomorphs, they are the natural restorers, the mirror image of the Gevurah Biome. It corresponds to Chesed, the fourth sephirah of the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, the head of the Right Pillar, the power of expansion and embrace. Gevurah halts destruction; Chesed appears only after destruction has ceased—repairing ruins, replanting broken branches, and stitching cracks through the life cycles of its biome species.
